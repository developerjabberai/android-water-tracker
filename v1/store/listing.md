**App name / Play title (30):** Widget: Water Tracker & Remind
**In-app and home-screen name:** Water Tracker
**Short description (80):** Water tracker widget: tap to log water. A little cat cheers you on.

**Full description (4000):**
Water Tracker is a water tracker widget for your home screen — no app to open, the widget is the whole app.

Tap it to log water. The bottle fills with a splash and gives a happy little bounce, with a cheerful cat-bottle character cheering you on.

WHAT YOU GET
- One tap logs half a glass or a full glass, your choice
- A dotted line marks where you should be by now, with a target dot colour-coded Behind, On Track, or Ahead — a hydration check at a glance
- See your progress in glasses right on the widget, e.g. 6.5/10
- A reminder that's impossible to miss: when you unlock your phone behind on water, the bottle gives a happy bounce, then shows exactly how much to drink
- A simple 7-day view in the app
- Everything stays on your device. No account, no ads, no tracking — the app doesn't even ask for internet access

HOW IT WORKS
Add the widget to your home screen in one tap, pick your daily hydration goal (from 5 to 20 glasses), and choose whether a tap logs half a glass or a full one. That's it.

A NOTE ON PERMISSIONS
Water Tracker uses a small background service so it can notice when you unlock your phone and remind you to drink. It shows one quiet notification ("Tap your widget to log water") you can hide any time. Nothing about your unlocks is stored or sent anywhere.

Water Tracker is not a medical app and gives no medical advice.
