# Play Store listing

**App name / Play title (30):** Widget: Water Tracker & Remind
**In-app and home-screen name:** Water Tracker
**Short description (80):** Water tracker widget: log water in one tap. A little cat cheers you on.

**Full description (4000):**
Water Tracker is a water tracker widget for your home screen, and the widget is the whole app.

Tap it to log water. The bottle fills up with a splash and does a happy little bounce. Every day a different cat-bottle character keeps you company, and a creeper vine grows up the bottle every day you reach your goal.

WHAT YOU GET
- One tap logs half a glass or a whole glass, your choice
- Four cute characters that take turns each day
- A dotted line shows where you should be by now, so you can see at a glance if you're ahead or behind
- Reach your goal (2 L, 3 L or 4 L) and your creeper grows a level, from wilted all the way to full bloom. Miss a day and it shrinks, so it's a streak you'll want to keep
- A reminder that's impossible to miss: when you unlock your phone and you're falling behind, the bottle bursts apart and rebuilds, then shows exactly how much to drink
- A simple 7-day view in the app
- Everything stays on your device. No account, no ads, no tracking, and the app doesn't even ask for internet access

HOW IT WORKS
Add the widget to your home screen in one tap from the app, pick your daily goal, and choose whether a tap logs half a glass or a full one. That's it.

A NOTE ON PERMISSIONS
To notice when you unlock your phone, the widget uses a small background service. It shows one quiet, low-priority notification ("Tap your widget to log water") that you can hide. Nothing about your unlocks is stored or sent anywhere.

Water Tracker is not a medical app and gives no medical advice.
