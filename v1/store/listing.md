**In-app and home-screen name:** Water Tracker
**Short description (80):** Water tracker widget: tap to log water. A little cat cheers you on.

**Full description (4000):**
Water Tracker is a water tracker widget for your home screen — the widget is the whole app.

Tap it to log water. The bottle fills with a splash and gives a happy little bounce. A different cat-bottle character keeps you company each day.

WHAT YOU GET
- One tap logs half a glass or a full glass, your choice
- Four cute characters that take turns, one each day
- A dotted line shows where you should be by now, so you can tell at a glance if you're ahead or behind
- A reminder that's impossible to miss when you're falling behind on water
- A simple 7-day view in the app
- Everything stays on your device. No account, no ads, no tracking — the app doesn't even ask for internet access

HOW IT WORKS
Add the widget to your home screen in one tap, pick your daily goal, and choose whether a tap logs half a glass or a full one. That's it.

A NOTE ON PERMISSIONS
Water Tracker uses a small background service so it can notice when you unlock your phone and remind you to drink. It shows one quiet notification you can hide any time. Nothing about your unlocks is stored or sent anywhere.

Water Tracker is not a medical app and gives no medical advice.
